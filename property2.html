Architectural Highlights

  • This residence is defined by its transparency and its relationship with the skyline. It prioritizes the "view as art."
  • Floor-to-Ceiling Fenestration: The use of expansive, seamless glass walls dissolves the boundary between the interior and the horizon, offering a 270-degree panoramic view of the water and the Detroit skyline.
  • Minimalist Structural Expression: Slim-profile mullions and a clean, white-on-white color palette emphasize a high-modernist aesthetic, allowing the architectural "bones" to remain subtle and unobtrusive.
  • Volumetric Lighting: The interior features a curated collection of sculptural pendant lighting - a mix of organic matte white and polished brass globes—which adds a layer of mid-century modern sophistication.
  • Integrated Outdoor Living: The transition to the glass-railed terrace is flush-mounted, extending the living square footage into the open air without visual interruption.
