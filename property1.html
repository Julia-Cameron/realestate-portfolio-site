Architectural Highlights

  • This estate utilizes European architectural traditions to create a commanding, prestigious presence.
  • Hand-Cut Limestone Facade: The exterior is clad in premium, light-toned limestone with precise masonry detailing. The quoining (cornerstones) and decorative window surrounds add depth and texture to the massive structure.
  • Mansard Roofline with Dormers: The slate-grey mansard roof is a quintessential French design element, featuring classic arched dormer windows that provide character and additional light to the upper-level suites.
  • Grand Portico Entry: A central, two-story limestone portico supported by elegant columns creates a formal "sense of arrival." This is topped with a semicircular arched pediment that anchors the home's symmetry.
  • Radiating Cobblestone Courtyard: The circular driveway features a European-style radiating cobblestone pattern, which contrasts beautifully with the sharp, geometric lines of the manicured boxwood parterre gardens.
  • Fenestration Hierarchy: The tall, arched windows on the ground floor draw the eye upward, while the uniform spacing of the casement windows across the facade reinforces the home's balanced, palatial scale.
