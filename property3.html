Architectural Highlights

  • This property is a study in "New Classical" modernism, utilizing heavy massing and symmetrical geometry to create a sense of permanent luxury.
  • Tiered Architectural Massing: The home is composed of interlocking rectangular volumes that create a rhythmic, stepped silhouette. This allows for various private wings and diverse outdoor experiences.
  • Linear Infinity Pool Design: The perimeter-overflow pool acts as a mirror for the home’s facade. The "zero-edge" design creates a visual bridge between the man-made structure and the natural body of water beyond.
  • Resort-Scale Hardscaping: The use of premium limestone for the expansive terraces, sunken fire-pit areas, and illuminated stair-treads provides a seamless, monochromatic look that glows during the golden hour.
  • Atrium-Style Great Room: The central "hub" of the home is a double-height glass atrium that serves as the architectural anchor, flooded with natural light and providing a grand sense of scale upon entry.
